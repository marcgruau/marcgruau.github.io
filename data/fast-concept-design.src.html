THE LABEL
INDEPENDENT ELECTRONIC LABEL
The space between sound and structure.

Founded by Marc Gruau, FAST CONCEPT is an independent label built where ambient electronic music meets a brutalist edge — and where automotive design is treated as part of the same language.

The identity treats the label as a structure rather than a logo: a fixed grid, a measured mark, and a typographic system that behaves like an engineering spec. Every release, poster and sleeve is cut from the same construction.

BRAND VALUES
THREE PRINCIPLES · ONE CONSTRUCTION
001
Concrete
as sound
MASS · WEIGHT · LOAD

A raw electronic sound that takes concrete and brutalist architecture as its structural reference. Mass, weight and load-bearing form translated into pressure and low end — music you feel through the floor before you hear it.

002
Nocturnal
discipline
LOOP · REPEAT · HOLD

Club culture treated as a practice, not a party. Rhythm is repetition, not ornament — a machined loop held long enough to become architecture in itself, and held again until the room agrees.

003
Measured
surface
SPEC · TOLERANCE · FIT

Automotive precision carried into design. Every surface is measured, every tolerance intentional; nothing is left to gesture or accident. The mark is placed, never arranged.

HOW THE GRID WAS BUILT

Checkerboard. Designed tiles and photography alternate, so no two flat graphics ever share an edge.

Two templates only. Releases carry the sleeve over a status band; type cards carry one number and one line.

Half photography. Six tiles show the label in the world — pressings, stickers, torn paper on concrete. Red in a photo never touches the red card.

Red once. One field-red card in twelve. Nucleus red only ever inside the mark.

Motion on the diagonal. The mark assembling, the turntable and FC001 spinning sit so no two moving tiles touch.

Built for the 3:4 grid. Posts are made at 1080 × 1350; type stays inside the profile crop.
